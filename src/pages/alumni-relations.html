The challenge

Engagement depends on trustworthy records.

Alumni, student, faculty, and staff data supports communications and event workflows. In a database of this size, imports, changes, and exceptions need consistent review so teams can rely on the records they use.

My contribution

Maintain quality through the daily workflow.

I execute bulk imports, validate records, resolve exceptions, apply tags, and update profiles. I use Excel reconciliation, data cleansing, and audience segmentation to support accurate stakeholder communications, event workflows, and alumni engagement processes.

Operating approach

Resolve exceptions before they spread.

The work combines routine processing with quality checks. Reconciliation and validation help surface discrepancies; exception resolution and tagging make the resulting data more usable for the teams that depend on it.

Management lens

Operational reliability comes from a repeatable process: clear inputs, quality checks, exception handling, and records ready for the next user.

Current outcome

A maintained engagement database.

This ongoing work supports the accuracy of the 61,000-profile database and the stakeholder communications and events that depend on it.
