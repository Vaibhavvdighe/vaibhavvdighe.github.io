The challenge

A national market required a first move.

For a South African actuarial firm considering U.S. expansion, leadership needed to decide where to focus initial research and entry effort. State-level opportunity, regulation, and public-sector context all mattered.

My ownership

Make the trade-offs comparable.

I developed a weighted state-scoring framework to organize the evidence and give leadership a defensible way to compare geographic options.

Decision approach

Show how the ranking was made.

I synthesized market, regulatory, and public-sector research into comparison criteria, applied weights, and used the results to prioritize opportunities. The visual above illustrates the structure without disclosing internal scores or client material.

Why weights matter

A ranking is more useful when leaders can see the assumptions behind it and challenge the trade-offs.

Outcome

A phased recommendation for leadership.

The analysis became a market-entry recommendation and proposed expansion roadmap presented to company leadership.

Reflection

A useful ranking makes its assumptions visible, so a team can challenge the trade-offs rather than accept a mysterious score.
