Choosing where to begin.

A weighted state-scoring framework helped turn a broad U.S. expansion question into prioritized geographic recommendations.

My roleMarket research and scoring framework
ClientZAQ Actuaries
FocusU.S. expansion priorities
01 · Situation

A large market is not one decision.

For a South African actuarial firm considering U.S. expansion, the first question was where to concentrate research and entry effort. The answer required comparing state level opportunity, regulation, and public sector context.

02 · Task

Create a defensible way to compare states.

I developed a weighted state-scoring framework to organize the evidence and make geographic trade-offs clearer for leadership.

03 · Action

Turn research into a structured choice.

I synthesized market, regulatory, and public sector research into comparison criteria, applied weights, and used the results to prioritize expansion opportunities. The visual above illustrates the decision structure; it does not disclose internal scores or client material.

04 · Result

A phased recommendation for leadership.

The analysis became a market entry recommendation and expansion roadmap presented to company leadership. This was a recommended path, not a claim that entry has already happened.

Reflection

A useful ranking makes its assumptions visible, so a team can challenge the trade-offs rather than accept a mysterious score.
