About

Curiosity with a purpose.

I work where business needs and technical systems meet. I enjoy clarifying the problem, organizing the work, and making the next decision easier for the people involved.

I grew up in Sangamner, India, and studied computer engineering in Pune. A concern about crop disease close to home made one technical challenge immediate. I proposed a detection capstone, helped develop the model, and owned the interface people would use.

That project shaped how I think about product work: a technical answer matters when it reaches a real user and supports a useful decision.

At the University of Oklahoma, I'm pursuing an MBA and an M.S. in Management of Information and Technology. In consulting work, I've led customer discovery and turned market research into recommendations. At Wastetide, I built a prioritization workflow used for CEO outreach and resolved a CRM data issue. In Alumni Relations, I manage ongoing data operations for a large engagement database.

I bring technical fluency to business questions and a practical interest in how work gets organized, evaluated, and used. I'm pursuing project, product, and program management roles where that combination is useful.
