About

Curiosity with a purpose.

I work at the meeting point of technical systems and human decisions. That perspective grew through engineering, business study, and projects where the answer had to be useful to someone else.

I grew up in Sangamner, India, and studied computer engineering in Pune. A concern about crop disease close to home made one technical challenge feel immediate. I proposed a plant disease detection capstone, helped develop the model, and owned the interface people would use to interact with it.

That project sharpened a question I still ask: what happens after a model or system works? A useful answer needs the right user experience, credible evidence, and a clear decision it supports.

At the University of Oklahoma, I'm pursuing an MBA and an M.S. in Management of Information Technology. In consulting work, I moved from technical execution into customer discovery, market analysis, and recommendations. At Wastetide, I brought those habits together in a Python based lead prioritization workflow and a production CRM audit.

Across these projects, the common thread is simple: I like turning messy inputs into a structure someone else can use. The technology matters, and so does the judgment about where to focus.
