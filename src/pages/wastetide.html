The challenge

Commercial focus was buried in the data.

Wastetide had a large universe of company and contact records. The immediate question was which accounts deserved the team's attention. A separate language-routing issue in HubSpot also made contact data less reliable for outreach.

My ownership

Give leadership a shortlist it could use.

As the Business Development and Data Analytics Intern, I translated business criteria into a repeatable prioritization process. I also investigated the CRM routing issue and corrected the affected assignments.

How I worked

Turn criteria into an operating workflow.

I developed a Python-based, weighted ideal customer profile model across 445,117 records. It qualified 18,318 companies, prioritized 3,915 prospects, and surfaced approximately 500 accounts for CEO outreach.

For the CRM issue, I automated an audit of approximately 15,000 HubSpot contacts and corrected more than 7,600 language assignments in the live system.

The workflow illustration is reconstructed for this portfolio; internal scoring criteria and records are omitted.
Decision approach

Make the output usable at the point of action.

The scoring model narrowed a broad market into an outreach list. The CRM audit addressed a different execution risk: whether the contact records would route to the right language when outreach began.

Two connected decisions

Who should receive attention first? Are the records reliable enough to act on that choice? Solving both made the recommendation more useful to leadership.

Outcome

A focused list and cleaner production data.

3,915prospects prioritized
~500accounts used by the CEO for outreach
7,600+CRM language assignments corrected

The account list informed outreach and the CRM corrections improved production data quality. Sales results were outside the scope of these reported outcomes.

Reflection

An analysis becomes valuable when the person making the next decision can trust and use it.

This work connected model design with the operational details that make a recommendation usable.
