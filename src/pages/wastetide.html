01 · Situation

Plenty of data, limited focus.

Wastetide had a large universe of company and contact records. The commercial challenge was deciding which accounts merited attention, while the CRM also needed more reliable language assignments for outreach.

02 · Task

Make the next outreach choice clearer.

My role was to translate business criteria into a usable lead prioritization process and identify data quality issues that could interfere with execution.

03 · Action

Build a decision workflow, then repair its inputs.

I developed a Python based, weighted ideal customer profile scoring model across 445,117 records. The workflow qualified 18,318 companies, prioritized 3,915 prospects, and surfaced approximately 500 accounts for CEO outreach.

Separately, I automated an audit of approximately 15,000 HubSpot contacts to diagnose a language routing issue and corrected more than 7,600 assignments in the live CRM.

The diagram is a portfolio reconstruction of the workflow. It does not expose Wastetide's internal scoring criteria or records.
04 · Result

A smaller list and a cleaner route to action.

3,915prospects prioritized

The account list informed outreach and the CRM corrections improved production data quality. These are workflow outputs; they are not claims of resulting sales.

Reflection

An analysis becomes valuable when the person making the next decision can trust and use it.

This work connected model design with the operational details that make a recommendation usable.
