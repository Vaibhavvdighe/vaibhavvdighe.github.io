The challenge

Clinical promise needed commercial evidence.

The team needed to understand customer needs, clinical workflows, market size, and the decisions required to move an innovation related to cisplatin-induced hearing loss toward market.

My ownership

Lead discovery and clarify the opportunity.

As an Associate Consultant, I owned customer discovery and helped translate the evidence into commercialization priorities for the client and consulting team.

How I worked

Connect interviews to a phased recommendation.

I led 15 stakeholder interviews and clinical research, then used those inputs to define customer needs and an estimated obtainable market of 57,220 patients. With the team, I developed a proposed 24-month roadmap covering competitive analysis, pricing and reimbursement, go-to-market planning, and potential partnerships.

Decision approach

Start with adoption conditions.

Customer discovery and clinical workflows shaped the opportunity before the team mapped the commercial steps. The roadmap organized future work into a sequence the client could evaluate.

What the roadmap connects

Customer needs → market opportunity → pricing and reimbursement → go-to-market and partnerships.

Outcome

A client-ready commercialization recommendation.

Reflection

A commercialization plan becomes stronger when it starts with the workflows people already live with.

The project made customer discovery central to how I think about the distance between invention and adoption.
