01 · Situation

A promising innovation needed a practical route forward.

Technical promise alone could not determine how ThermoGel might reach the people and institutions involved in care for cisplatin induced hearing loss. The team needed evidence about customer needs, clinical workflows, market size, and commercialization choices.

02 · Task

Understand the user and the market.

I owned customer discovery and helped translate what we learned into commercialization priorities for the client and the broader consulting team.

03 · Action

Move from conversations to a structured plan.

I led 15 stakeholder interviews and clinical research, then used those inputs to define customer needs and an estimated obtainable market of 57,220 patients. With the team, I developed a 24 month execution roadmap covering competitive analysis, pricing and reimbursement, go-to-market planning, and potential partnerships.

04 · Result

A recommendation the client could evaluate.

57,220patients in the obtainable-market estimate
24 monthscovered by the execution roadmap

We presented the recommendations to the client. The patient figure is a market estimate, not a count of people treated, and the roadmap describes a proposed path rather than an implemented launch.

Reflection

A commercialization plan becomes stronger when it starts with the workflows people already live with.

The project made customer discovery central to how I think about the distance between invention and adoption.
