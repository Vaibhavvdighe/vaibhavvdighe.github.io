The challenge

AI adoption needed clear boundaries.

New AI-enabled workflows can create value, but they also raise questions about what information can be used, who can access it, and how client confidentiality is protected. The organization needed a consistent way to evaluate those questions before wider adoption.

My contribution

Turn risks into usable rules.

I developed an enterprise AI governance and data policy covering data security, acceptable use, access management, client confidentiality, and responsible AI use. I assessed operational and information-security risks across potential AI-enabled workflows and translated them into a risk-management framework.

Decision approach

Make the next review more consistent.

The framework connects each potential use case to the data it touches, the risk it creates, and the control needed before it proceeds. That structure helps a decision-maker ask the same essential questions across different workflows.

Management lens

The value of governance is consistency: a team can compare opportunities while preserving clear expectations for security, access, and confidentiality.

Outcome

A framework for future decisions.

I delivered a policy and risk-management framework to guide future AI adoption and governance decisions. Implementation remains a future decision for the organization.
